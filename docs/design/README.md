# Design reference

The screens from the TargetVideo design file that this prototype builds, named by what they show. The renders (`*.jpg`) stay local and are not committed: the design belongs to TargetVideo and the app is not released yet.

| Screen | Shows | Built |
|---|---|---|
| `home` | Home feed: hero, genre chips, rows of series, tab bar | Yes; Play and the posters open the series at the last episode watched |
| `episode-feed` | Vertical feed of episode cards, locked from episode 5 | Yes |
| `continue-the-romance` | Unlock choice: episode, full season, premium | Episode and season only |
| `coin-packs` | Locked episode, balance and coin packs | Without the VIP Membership block and App Store Pay |
